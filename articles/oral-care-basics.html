Oral Care Basics: Brushing, Flossing & Fresh Breath

The 2-minute brushing standard, fluoride facts, interdental cleaning and what actually fixes bad breath.

BCBody.Care Editorial Team
Editorial & review policyUpdated Sep 19, 20265 min read2 cited sources

The daily standard

  • Brush twice a day for 2 minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Spit, don't rinse after brushing so fluoride stays on teeth.
  • Clean between teeth once a day — floss, interdental brushes or a water flosser.
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3–4 months.

Bad breath

Most bad breath comes from bacteria on the tongue and between teeth. Brush or scrape your tongue, clean between teeth, stay hydrated, and treat gum disease. Mouthwash masks odour temporarily.

Gums

Bleeding gums when brushing are often an early sign of gum inflammation (gingivitis) — keep cleaning gently, not less. Book a dental check if it persists beyond two weeks.

🩺 When to see a doctorSee a dentist for tooth pain, swelling, persistent bleeding gums, loose teeth or a mouth ulcer lasting over 3 weeks.

FAQ

Electric or manual brush?
Both work with good technique. Powered brushes can remove more plaque for many people.
Is whitening safe?
Dentist-supervised or reputable peroxide products are generally safe; sensitivity is common and temporary.
